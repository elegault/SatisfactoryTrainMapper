# Left and Right Build Modes Explained

**Q: Is there a difference in terminology between the "Right Side" and "Left Side" build modes for signals vs. the placement of the signal on the track's left or right?**

**A: Great question — and yes, this is one of the most confusing parts of Satisfactory’s signaling system.**

**You’ve run into the difference between:**

1. **The *build mode* (“Right Side” / “Left Side”)**  
2. **The *actual side of the track* the signal ends up on**

These are *not the same thing*, and the naming is unfortunately misleading.

Let’s break it down cleanly.

---

✅ **1. “Right Side / Left Side” build mode = which *rail* the signal snaps to**
This setting controls **which rail of the track** the signal attaches to — not which *physical side* of the track it appears on.

- **Right Side build mode** = attach to the *right-hand rail*  
- **Left Side build mode** = attach to the *left-hand rail*

But depending on the curve, slope, or merge geometry, the *rail* you attach to may physically be on the left or right side of the world.

---

✅ **2. What actually matters: the signal must face the train on its *right-hand side***  
Satisfactory trains follow **right-hand traffic**.

So the rule is:

> **A signal must be placed on the train’s right-hand side *relative to its direction of travel*.**

This is the *only* rule the game cares about.

If the geometry of a merge makes the “right-hand rail” physically appear on the left side of the track, that’s fine — the game still considers it the correct side.

---

🎯 **How to verify you placed the signal correctly**
Stand on the track and face the direction the train will travel.

- If the signal is on **your right**, it’s correct  
- If it’s on **your left**, the train will ignore it  

Ignore the build mode labels — trust the *final position*.

---

🛠️ **Practical tip for merges**
At a merge:

- Put a **path signal** on the *right-hand side* of each incoming track  
- If the game forces you to place it on the “wrong-looking” side, that’s because the rail geometry twists  
- As long as the signal ends up on the train’s right-hand side, it’s valid
